AWS and QuEra announced an expanded collaboration to deliver fault-tolerant quantum computers via Amazon Braket. The partnership will introduce Libra, a megaquop-scale device capable of executing one million quantum operations across hundreds of logical qubits, available by 2028. The advance targets scientifically relevant applications in quantum chemistry, high-energy physics, and materials simulation beyond classical computing capabilities.
